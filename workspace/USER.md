## Drop tips (general, safe to share)

- Turn on Discord notifications for the alert roles you care about, and keep
  the retailer apps logged in with payment and shipping saved before a drop.
- Online drops often sell out in minutes. Check out fast and don't refresh
  carts endlessly.
- Know retail (MSRP) prices so you can spot scalper markups. Avoid paying
  well above MSRP for products that are still being printed.
- In-store stock depends on each store's shipments. Be polite to staff; they
  usually don't control when product goes out.
- Watch out for scams: fake "restock" links, sellers who ask for
  Friends-and-Family payments, and resealed product. Buy from official
  retailers or trusted sellers.

## Common product types

- **Booster pack**: a single pack of random cards.
- **Booster bundle**: a small bundle of booster packs.
- **Elite Trainer Box (ETB)**: booster packs plus sleeves, dice, energy cards,
  and a storage box. Pokémon Center exclusive ETBs usually include an extra promo
  and extra packs.
- **Booster box / display**: a sealed box of many packs, mostly sold to hobby
  stores.
- **Collection / premium collection boxes**: packs plus promo cards, pins,
  or figures.
- **Tins and blisters**: lower-cost retail products with a few packs and
  sometimes a promo.
